## What is this study about?
You will play a short map-based decision game together with three other participants. The game consists of three rounds.

## What data is collected?
- Your actions in the game (which areas you propose or decide on) and when you take them.
- How you use the interface (e.g. which areas you look at, how long info panels are open, whether the game window is in focus).
- Your answers to a few background questions (age bracket, field of study, experience).
<!-- if audio -->
- **A recording of your microphone**: your voice and what is said in the room (see *Audio recording* below).
<!-- end if -->

**No names, e-mail addresses, IP addresses or device identifiers are stored** by the game. Your data are stored under a random code that is shown to you at the end of the game.
<!-- if audio -->
Of your microphone, only its technical settings are stored (its sample rate and whether the browser filtered the sound), never its name or id. If you or others say a name aloud, it is in the recording.
<!-- end if -->

## Audio recording
- **When:** your browser records your microphone from the microphone check (right after this form) until the game is complete: during the background questions, the briefing, the calibration, all rounds, breaks and game pauses. You cannot mute yourself. The researcher can pause the recording for everyone, for example when someone else enters the room.
- **No microphone opens before everyone has agreed.** The microphone checks start only once every participant in the room has answered this form.
- **Other voices:** all participants sit in the same room, so your microphone also picks up the others, and theirs pick up you.
- **Calibration:** before the first round, each participant reads a short sentence aloud in turn while the others stay quiet. This measures how loud each voice is on each microphone.

## How is the data protected?
Data are stored encrypted on the researcher's computer and are never uploaded to the internet during the session. Results are reported only in aggregate or under the random code.

## Withdrawal
You may stop at any time. After the session you can ask for your data to be removed by quoting your code to the contact address. Once data have been fully anonymised (see retention below) they can no longer be linked to you and can therefore no longer be removed.
