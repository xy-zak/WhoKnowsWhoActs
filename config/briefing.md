# How to play

> **PLACEHOLDER TEXT** — replace with your game briefing.

- Everyone sees the **same map outline**, but **the information inside each area differs per player**.
- Click an area to see what *you* know about it.
- Use **Propose** to highlight an area for everyone (amber).
- Use **Decide** to mark an area as decided (blue).
- Each round you have a limited number of proposals and decisions — the counters are at the top.
- The round ends when everyone has used all their actions. There are three rounds.
