# How to play

> **PLACEHOLDER TEXT** — replace with your game briefing.

- The map is a grid of **squares**. Every square has four **sub-squares**, one per knowledge type (Ka, Kb, Kc, Kd), each with a score from 0 to 10.
- You only see the scores of **your own knowledge type**. The other players see other scores. The **map score** at the top is the average of all scores.
- Click a square to see what *you* know about it.
- Your proposals and decisions always land on the sub-square of **your power type**, in the square you choose.
- **Propose** marks that sub-square for everyone (hatched). It changes no scores.
- **Decide** fills it for everyone and changes the scores of that square: its score goes up, the other three sub-squares of the square go down.
- Each round you have a limited number of proposals and decisions. The counters and the round number are at the top.
- A round ends when everyone has used all their actions, or when the facilitator ends it.
